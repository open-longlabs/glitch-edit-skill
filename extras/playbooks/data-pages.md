# Data pages, poster type, thermal scan

- Pages are minimal mono type, only TRUE facts (product fact sheet, specs the client gave you), few lines, one joke line for people who pause ("collab status: pending", "note to the brand: plz collab."). Never a report page.
- Kinds: receipt (toner body on paper + table + barcode), spec label, dark spec card, barcode request page, technical sketch with true callouts.
- Poster type behind the subject (Nike style): Futura Condensed ExtraBold, size fitted to the free side, text layer composited UNDER the person mask. Put it on a shot where the subject sits on one side (profile), 6 frames. Lines that worked: "JUST DO IT."; "PLZ COLLAB NIKE." only as low-key small print.
- Thermal scan (shades shield): ironbow palette, skin warm, lens region = darkest area in the head band (percentile, relative to the face, ellipse close) forced cold, two crosshair readouts (lens vs skin), °C scale bar, "values illustrative".
Script: `../reference/spec-ad-cut.py` (P_shades_receipt, P_shades_hud = thermal, P_shoe_spec, P_shoe_dark, P_barcode, P_sketch, P_roto).
